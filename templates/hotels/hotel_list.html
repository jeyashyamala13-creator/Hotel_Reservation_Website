Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my booking? ▼

You can easily cancel your booking by going to "My Dashboard" after logging in. Click the "Cancel Stay" button next to your active booking.

Are there any hidden charges? ▼

No, the price you see on the checkout page is the final price. We believe in 100% pricing transparency.

What are the standard Check-In and Check-Out timings? ▼

Generally, standard hotel check-in starts from 12:00 PM onwards, and check-out must be completed before 11:00 AM on the day of your departure.

Is it mandatory to create an account to reserve rooms? ▼

Yes, creating a secured account helps protect your transaction history and allows you to access the Cancellation engine at any given time via your profile dashboard.

How long does a refund take after a successful cancellation? ▼

Once you hit cancel on your dashboard, processing happens instantly. The bank settlement will credit back your original source payment route within 5 to 7 working business days.
